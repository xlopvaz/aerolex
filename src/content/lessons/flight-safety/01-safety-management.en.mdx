## The SMS

A <Term id="sms" /> is the organised way in which an operator, an airport or a maintenance organisation finds its hazards and controls its risks. ICAO Annex 19 structures it in **four components**:

| Component | What it includes |
|---|---|
| **Policy and objectives** | Management commitment, accountable executive, responsibilities, measurable objectives |
| **Risk management** | Identifying hazards, assessing and reducing risks |
| **Safety assurance** | Measuring performance, managing change, continuous improvement |
| **Safety promotion** | Training and communication |

## Hazard and risk

- A <Term id="hazard" /> is something that **could** cause harm: birds near the runway, a short wet runway, a confusing chart.
- **Risk** is the assessment of its consequences: **how likely** they are and **how severe** they would be.

### The risk matrix

ICAO proposes rating probability from 1 to 5 and severity from A to E:

| Probability | | Severity | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Frequent | A | Catastrophic |
| 4 | Occasional | B | Hazardous |
| 3 | Remote | C | Major |
| 2 | Improbable | D | Minor |
| 1 | Extremely improbable | E | Negligible |

The index combines both (for example **4B**). Depending on where it falls in the matrix, the risk is **intolerable** (act before continuing), **tolerable** with mitigation, or **acceptable**.

## How hazards are found

| Method | Example |
|---|---|
| **Reactive** | Investigating an event that has already happened |
| **Proactive** | Analysing voluntary reports, audits, surveys |
| **Predictive** | Analysing trends in large amounts of data, such as flight data |

Without **reporting** there is no SMS. That is why <Term id="just-culture" /> is key: honest error is not punished, but gross negligence or deliberate acts are.

## Flight data monitoring

EU rules require commercial operators of aeroplanes with a maximum certificated take-off mass above **27,000 kg** to run a <Term id="fdm" /> programme, integrated in their management system. The recorded data from **all** flights are analysed to detect deviations: unstabilised approaches, hard landings, excessive speeds…

The programme must be **non-punitive** and protect crew identity: its purpose is to find trends and fix them, not to find someone to blame.

<Callout type="info" label="Safety is measured">
An SMS sets indicators (for example, unstabilised approaches per thousand flights) and targets. That shows whether the measures are working.
</Callout>
