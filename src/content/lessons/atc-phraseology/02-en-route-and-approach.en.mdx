## Levels

| Instruction | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "Climb flight level 240" | Climb to FL240 |
| "Descend altitude 3000 feet, QNH 1013" | Descend to 3,000 ft on QNH 1013 |
| "Maintain flight level 240" | Maintain FL240 |
| "Stop climb at flight level 200" | Stop the climb at FL200 |
| "Report reaching flight level 240" | Report on reaching FL240 |
| "Expedite climb" | Climb at the best possible vertical speed |

When moving from flight levels to altitudes, ATC gives the <Term id="qnh" /> and it is read back.

## Headings, speeds and routings

- "**Turn left heading 250**"; "**continue present heading**"; "**fly heading 090**".
- "**Proceed direct** TOBEK"; "**resume own navigation**" when <Term id="vectoring" /> ends.
- "**Reduce speed to 220 knots**"; "**maintain 250 knots or greater**"; "**no speed restriction**". Speeds are kept until ATC cancels them.

## Holding

> "Iberia 3456, hold at TOBEK, inbound track 270 degrees, right-hand pattern, expect further clearance at 1035."

For a <Term id="holding" />, ATC gives the fix, the inbound track, the direction of turns if not standard and the time at which further clearance is expected.

## Approach and landing

> **ATC**: "Iberia 3456, turn right heading 330, descend altitude 4000 feet, cleared ILS approach runway 36 right, report established."
> **Pilot**: "Right heading 330, descend altitude 4000 feet, cleared ILS approach runway 36 right, wilco, Iberia 3456."
> **ATC**: "Iberia 3456, runway 36 right, wind 350 degrees 12 knots, **cleared to land**."

If the runway is not yet clear, ATC may say "**continue approach**", which is not a landing clearance.

### Go-around

- Ordered by ATC: "Iberia 3456, **go around**, I say again, go around" → pilot: "**Going around**".
- Decided by the pilot: "**Going around**" as soon as able, without waiting.

Then the published <Term id="missed-approach" /> or ATC's instructions are followed.

## TCAS and wake

If <Term id="tcas" /> gives a resolution advisory, the crew **follows it**, even if it contradicts an ATC instruction, and reports it as soon as possible:

> **Pilot**: "Iberia 3456, **TCAS RA**."

ATC stops giving that aircraft instructions in the vertical plane. When it is over:

> **Pilot**: "Iberia 3456, **clear of conflict, returning to** flight level 240."

Behind a heavier aircraft, ATC may warn: "**Caution wake turbulence**".

<Callout type="info" label="Clear instructions">
Each instruction puts the key word first (heading, flight level, altitude, speed) and then the numbers. That way a heading is not confused with a level.
</Callout>
