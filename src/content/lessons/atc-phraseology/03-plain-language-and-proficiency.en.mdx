## When phraseology is not enough

Phraseology covers routine situations, but not all of them: a sick passenger, an animal on the runway, an unusual failure. Then **plain language** is used, with these guidelines:

- **short** sentences in logical order;
- **simple** words, without idioms or local slang;
- **one idea** per sentence;
- **numbers** spoken as in phraseology;
- check that the other party has **understood**.

> "Iberia 3456, we have a passenger with chest pain. Request priority landing and medical assistance on arrival."

Emergencies above all must be **unambiguous**: "MAYDAY" or "PAN PAN" is better than "we have a little problem".

## CPDLC

<Term id="cpdlc" /> lets pilot and controller exchange standard text messages: level clearances, frequency changes, direct routings. It is used mainly en route and in oceanic areas, and it **complements** voice rather than replacing it.

Responses to a message requiring a reply are standardised:

| Response | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **WILCO** | Will comply |
| **UNABLE** | Cannot comply |
| **STANDBY** | Wait, will reply |
| **ROGER** | Received |
| **AFFIRM** / **NEGATIVE** | Yes / No |

At any doubt or urgency, switch to **voice**.

## Language proficiency

Pilots and controllers who use the radio in international operations must demonstrate <Term id="language-proficiency" /> in the language they use, usually English. ICAO defines a **six-level** scale:

| Level | Name |
|---|---|
| 1 | Pre-elementary |
| 2 | Elementary |
| 3 | Pre-operational |
| **4** | **Operational** (minimum required) |
| 5 | Extended |
| 6 | Expert |

Six areas are assessed: **pronunciation, structure, vocabulary, fluency, comprehension and interactions**. The overall rating is the **lowest** of the six.

### Validity

| Level | ICAO (recommended) | EU (FCL.055) |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Every 3 years | Every **4 years** |
| 5 | Every 6 years | Every **6 years** |
| 6 | No re-evaluation | No re-evaluation |

Proficiency is endorsed on the licence with its validity date.

<Callout type="tip" label="Aviation English is not just phraseology">
Level 4 also requires handling unexpected situations in plain language. Practising with real recordings and describing aloud what happens in flight helps a lot.
</Callout>
