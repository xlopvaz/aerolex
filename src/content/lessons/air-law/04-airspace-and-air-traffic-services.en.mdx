## Air traffic services

There are three air traffic services:

- **Air traffic control** (ATC): preventing collisions and keeping traffic orderly, through **clearances** and instructions.
- **Flight information** (FIS): giving information useful for a safe flight: weather, aerodrome status, other known traffic.
- **Alerting**: notifying search and rescue services when an aircraft needs help.

<Term id="atc" /> is provided by three kinds of unit: the **tower** (TWR) at the aerodrome, **approach control** (APP) in <Term id="tma" />s and around aerodromes, and the **area control centre** (ACC) en route.

## Regions and zones

- An <Term id="fir" /> (flight information region) covers a large area, often a country; at least flight information and alerting are provided in it.
- A <Term id="ctr" /> (control zone) surrounds one or more aerodromes from the surface upwards.
- A **control area** (CTA), such as a TMA, starts at a set height above the ground.

## Airspace classes

Each portion of airspace has a <Term id="airspace-class">class</Term>, from A to G:

| Class | Flights allowed | ATC separation |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | IFR only | Between all |
| **B** | IFR and VFR | Between all |
| **C** | IFR and VFR | IFR from IFR and from VFR; VFR from IFR. Between VFR, traffic information |
| **D** | IFR and VFR | IFR from IFR. Others get traffic information |
| **E** | IFR and VFR | IFR from IFR. VFR flights need no clearance |
| **F** | IFR and VFR | Advisory service for IFR flights that request it |
| **G** | IFR and VFR | None: flight information only |

A to E is **controlled airspace**; F and G are uncontrolled.

## The speed limit

In classes **C** (for VFR), **D, E, F and G**, below **FL100**, the maximum speed is **250 kt IAS**, unless the authority approves otherwise in particular cases. It is one of the reasons jets slow down when descending below 10,000 ft.

## Special areas

| Area | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Prohibited** (P) | Flight is not allowed |
| **Restricted** (R) | Flight is allowed only under certain conditions |
| **Danger** (D) | There may be activities dangerous to flight, at published times |
| <Term id="rmz" /> | A radio is mandatory, with listening watch or contact |
| <Term id="tmz" /> | A transponder with altitude reporting is mandatory |

All are published in the <Term id="aip" /> and on charts, and their activation may be announced by <Term id="notam" />.
