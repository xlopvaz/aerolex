<Callout type="official-procedure" label="The operator's manual prevails">
Operating each door (arming, disarming, normal and emergency opening) differs on every aircraft type and is learned in type training, with practice on the aircraft or in a door trainer.
</Callout>

## Types of exits

- **Floor-level exits:** the boarding doors and other service or emergency doors. They have an <Term id="evacuation-slide" /> if the sill is high enough above the ground.
- **Overwing exits:** emergency windows leading onto the wing. From there, occupants get down via the wing's edge or a slide, depending on the aircraft.
- Certification rules (for example FAA section 25.807) classify exits by **type** according to their size and position; the larger the exit, the more passengers it can evacuate.

## Armed and disarmed

A door with a slide has two modes:

| Mode | What happens when it is opened | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Armed** | The slide deploys and inflates automatically | From before pushback until arrival at the stand |
| **Disarmed** | It opens like a normal door | On the ground, for boarding and disembarking |

That is why, when the doors are closed before departure and on arrival, the crew **arm or disarm** each door and carry out a **cross-check**: another crew member verifies that each door is in the correct mode. Opening an armed door by mistake at the stand deploys the slide and can injure anyone outside.

## Evacuating in 90 seconds

To certify a transport aircraft with **more than 44 seats**, the manufacturer must show that all occupants can get out **within 90 seconds**, under simulated emergency conditions, using **no more than half** of the exits in the sides of the fuselage. That demonstration, with representative passengers and crew, is one of the bases for the minimum number of cabin crew.

## Before opening an exit in an emergency

Although every operator has its own procedure, there is always one step first: **assess the conditions outside** (fire, smoke, water, obstacles, the aircraft's attitude). A blocked or dangerous exit is not opened, and passengers are redirected to another one.

</Callout>
