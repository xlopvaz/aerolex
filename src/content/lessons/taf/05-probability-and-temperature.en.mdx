## Probability: PROB30 and PROB40

When the forecaster is not sure a phenomenon will occur, its **probability** is given. Only two values are used: **30 %** and **40 %**. (Below 30 % it is not considered significant enough to include; from 50 % it is simply forecast.)

`PROB` can appear:

- **on its own**, with a period: `PROB30 1312/1316 TSRA BKN015CB`
- **combined with TEMPO**: `PROB40 TEMPO 1314/1318 +SHRA`

It is not combined with `BECMG` or `FM`.

<MetarBreakdown parts={[
  ["PROB30", "30 % probability…"],
  ["TEMPO 1312/1316", "…that, temporarily between 12 and 16 UTC on day 13,"],
  ["TSRA BKN015CB", "there will be a thunderstorm with rain and cumulonimbus at 1,500 ft"]
]} />

## Temperatures: TX and TN

Some TAFs include, at the end of the base section, the forecast **maximum (`TX`)** and **minimum (`TN`)** temperatures, with their times:

<MetarBreakdown parts={[
  ["TX24/1315Z", "Maximum 24 °C, day 13 at 15 UTC"],
  ["TN07/1306Z", "Minimum 7 °C, day 13 at 06 UTC"],
  ["TNM02/1406Z", "Minimum −2 °C, day 14 at 06 UTC"]
]} />

They help anticipate take-off performance in hot weather or ice and frost risk in cold weather.

## NSW: end of significant weather

`NSW` (*no significant weather*) is used in a change group to indicate that the significant weather forecast earlier **stops**:

`BECMG 1316/1318 30010KT 9999 NSW SCT025`

<Callout type="regional-diff" label="ICAO / US">
US TAFs rely mostly on `FM` groups, and probability is only expressed as `PROB30`.
</Callout>
