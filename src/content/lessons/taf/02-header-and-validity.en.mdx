## The header

```
TAF LEMD 121700Z 1218/1324
```

| Group | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `TAF` | Message type |
| `LEMD` | ICAO location indicator of the aerodrome |
| `121700Z` | Issued: day 12, 17:00 UTC (same format as in a METAR) |
| `1218/1324` | Valid: from day 12 at 18 UTC to day 13 at 24 UTC |

## The validity period: `DDHH/DDHH`

Unlike the issue time, validity uses **day and hour, without minutes**. The end `1324` means "day 13 at 24 UTC", the same as day 14 at 00 UTC: it is written this way to make clear that the period ends at the end of day 13.

<MetarBreakdown parts={[
  ["1306/1406", "From day 13 at 06 UTC to day 14 at 06 UTC: 24 hours"],
  ["1312/1418", "From day 13 at 12 UTC to day 14 at 18 UTC: 30 hours"],
  ["1309/1318", "From day 13 at 09 UTC to day 13 at 18 UTC: 9 hours"]

## Amendments, corrections and cancellations

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `TAF AMD` | **Amended** TAF: the forecast has changed and this one replaces the previous one |
| `TAF COR` | **Corrected** TAF: corrects an error in the previous one |
| `CNL` | **Cancelled** TAF |
| `NIL` | No TAF available |

<Callout type="tip" label="Tip">
When checking a TAF, always make sure you are reading **the latest one issued** (and whether there is an `AMD`). An amended TAF completely replaces the previous one.
</Callout>
