## Method: draw a timeline

1. Read the **header**: aerodrome, issue time and validity period.
2. Note the **base conditions**: they apply from the start.
3. Go through the **change groups** in order and place them on the timeline.
4. For a given time, ask: which `FM` or `BECMG` has already happened? Is any `TEMPO` or `PROB` active?

All the TAFs in this lesson are **didactic examples**.

## 1. Madrid: a quiet night with temporary mist

```
TAF LEMD 121700Z 1218/1324 24012KT 9999 SCT030 TX24/1315Z TN07/1306Z
    BECMG 1220/1222 VRB03KT
    TEMPO 1303/1308 3000 BR BKN008
    BECMG 1310/1312 27015KT
```
