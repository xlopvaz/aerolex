## Cabin baggage

Regulation (EU) No 965/2012 requires that only baggage that can be **adequately and securely stowed** is taken into the cabin. In practice:

- in the overhead bins or under the seat in front, never in the aisle or next to an exit;
- bins closed and not overloaded;
- in emergency exit rows, nothing on the floor that could get in the way of an evacuation.

## What a secured cabin means

Before taxi, take-off and landing, the crew check, among other things, that:

- every passenger is **seated with their seat belt fastened**, and infants are restrained according to the operator's procedure;
- **seat backs** are upright and **tray tables** stowed;
- **baggage** is stowed and aisles and exits are **clear**;
- **galleys** are secured and carts locked;
- **lavatories** are unoccupied;
- the crew are in their seats with their harnesses fastened.

When everything is ready, the senior cabin crew member reports it to the flight deck using the operator's phrase.

<Callout type="info" label="Why so much detail">
The first and last minutes of a flight concentrate a lot of risk. An open tray table or a bag in the aisle can cost vital seconds in an evacuation.
</Callout>
