## The ICAO flight plan

The <Term id="flight-plan" /> tells air traffic services what you are going to do. Its format is set by ICAO procedures (PANS-ATM). The most important items:

| Item | Content | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **7** | Aircraft identification | IBE3456 |
| **8** | Flight rules (I, V, Y, Z) and type of flight (S scheduled, N non-scheduled, G general, M military, X other) | IS |
| **9** | Aircraft type and wake turbulence category (L, M, H, J) | A320/M |
| **10** | Communication, navigation and surveillance equipment | SDFGRY/SB1 |
| **13** | Departure aerodrome and estimated off-block time | LEMD0730 |
| **15** | Speed, level and route | N0450F350 … |
| **16** | Destination, total estimated elapsed time and alternates | GCXO0230 GCTS |
| **18** | Other information (PBN, registration…) | PBN/A1B1D1 REG/ECABC |
| **19** | Supplementary information: endurance, persons on board, emergency equipment | |

In item 15, **N0450F350** means **450 knots** true airspeed at **FL350**.

## When it is filed

Under the European rules of the air, a flight plan for a flight that will receive air traffic control service must be submitted **at least 60 minutes before** departure. In Europe, IFR flight plans are processed centrally by the Network Manager, which also assigns, if needed, a **take-off slot** to regulate traffic.

## The pre-flight briefing

Before departure the crew reviews:

- the **operational flight plan**: route, levels, times and fuel;
- the **weather**: METARs and TAFs for departure, destination and alternates, significant weather charts, upper winds and temperatures, and SIGMETs;
- <Term id="notam" />s: closed runways, navaids out of service, activated areas;
- the **aircraft status**: items deferred under the minimum equipment list and their effect on performance;
- the planned **mass and balance**.

With all of this, the crew decides whether the flight can go as planned, chooses the **alternates** and sets the **fuel**.

<Callout type="tip" label="Reading a NOTAM">
A NOTAM says what changes, where and when. The essentials are in line E (the text) and the validity dates. A NOTAM closing a runway can change the fuel, the alternate and even whether the flight is viable.
</Callout>
