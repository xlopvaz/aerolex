## The components

EU operating rules require the fuel for a commercial air transport flight to include at least:

| Component | For |
|---|---|
| **Taxi** | Start-up, taxiing and APU use before take-off |
| <Term id="trip-fuel" /> | From take-off to landing at the destination |
| <Term id="contingency-fuel" /> | The unexpected: wind, routing or level different from plan. Normally **5 % of trip fuel**, with a minimum of **5 minutes' holding at 1,500 ft** above the destination |
| **Alternate** | From destination to the <Term id="alternate-aerodrome" />: go-around, climb, cruise, descent and approach |
| <Term id="final-reserve-fuel" /> | For turbine aircraft, **30 minutes' holding at 1,500 ft** above the aerodrome, in standard conditions |
| **Additional** | When the above is not enough for specific cases, such as an engine or pressurisation failure at the most critical point of a route over water |
| **Extra** | Whatever the operator or commander decides above the minimum: forecast storms, usual holding |

The **final reserve** is the last line of defence: it is not planned to be used.

## An example

A flight with these figures:

| Component | kg |
|---|---|
| Taxi | 200 |
| Trip | 6,000 |
| Contingency (5 % of trip) | 300 |
| Alternate | 1,400 |
| Final reserve (30 min) | 1,100 |
| **Minimum take-off fuel** | **8,800** (excluding taxi) |
| **Minimum ramp fuel** | **9,000** |

If the commander adds 500 kg of extra for forecast storms, they will load **9,500 kg**.

## Monitoring fuel in flight

During the flight, actual fuel is compared with the planned figures at points along the route. If the predicted landing fuel drops, the crew re-plans: a more direct route, a closer alternate or a diversion.

There are two calls to ATC, standardised by ICAO:

- <Term id="minimum-fuel">"MINIMUM FUEL"</Term>: the aircraft has committed to land at a specific aerodrome and **any change** to its clearance could make it land with **less than final reserve**. It is **not** an emergency, but it tells ATC it cannot accept more delay.
- **"MAYDAY, MAYDAY, MAYDAY, FUEL"**: calculated fuel on landing at the nearest suitable aerodrome is **less than final reserve**. It is an **emergency**.

<Callout type="caution" label="Don't wait until the last minute">
Both calls exist so that ATC can help in time. Declaring too late reduces the options.
</Callout>
