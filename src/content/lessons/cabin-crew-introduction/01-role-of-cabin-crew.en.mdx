<Callout type="official-procedure" label="The operator's manual prevails">
This course explains the general European rules. In real operations, the procedures, figures and duties that apply are those in your airline's operations manual.
</Callout>

## Safety first

<Term id="cabin-crew">Cabin crew</Term> are on board, above all, **for the safety of passengers**. Service (meals, drinks, onboard sales) matters to the airline, but the reason the rules require cabin crew is to have trained people who can:

- check the emergency equipment and prepare the cabin for each phase of flight;
- brief <Term id="pax">passengers</Term> on safety and make sure the rules are followed;
- respond to incidents such as smoke, fire, turbulence, an ill or disruptive passenger;
- lead an **evacuation** if one becomes necessary.

## When are cabin crew required?

In the European Union, Regulation (EU) No 965/2012 (point ORO.CC.100) requires **at least one cabin crew member** on aircraft with a maximum operational passenger seating configuration of **more than 19** when carrying one or more passengers.

## How many are needed?

The minimum number is the **greater** of:

1. the number of cabin crew established during the **aircraft's certification** for the cabin configuration the operator uses (linked to the manufacturer's evacuation demonstration);
2. **one cabin crew member for every 50 passenger seats, or fraction of 50**, installed on the same deck.

EASA explains that, for aircraft whose certification documentation was issued after 3 July 2017, the operator must apply the minimum number stated in that documentation.

When more than one cabin crew member is on board, the operator nominates one of them to be responsible to the commander: the <Term id="senior-cabin-crew-member">senior cabin crew member</Term>.

<Callout type="info" label="More than the minimum">
Airlines may carry more cabin crew than required, for example because of the type of service or the length of the flight. The minimum is a safety figure, not a service roster.
</Callout>
