## An eight-step method

1. **What, where and when?** Type, aerodrome and UTC time. Is it recent?
2. **Wind:** direction, speed, gusts and variation.
3. **Visibility and RVR:** are low-visibility operations involved?
4. **Present weather:** any hazardous phenomena?
5. **Cloud:** where is the ceiling? Any CB or TCU?
6. **Temperature and dew point:** fog or icing risk?
7. **QNH.**
8. **Supplementary and trend:** improving or deteriorating?

All the reports in this lesson are **didactic examples**: their format is valid, but they are not real observations.
